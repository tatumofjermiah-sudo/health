Teachings for a New Society

01

Root Causes, Not Symptoms

Western medicine treats symptoms, not causes. Pharmaceuticals suppress symptoms while the underlying condition continues or worsens. True healing requires identifying and eliminating the root cause — most often, what we eat, drink, breathe, and think.

02

You Are the Environment You Live In

Your body is an environment — and every environment responds to its inputs. A garden grows what you plant. A river flows clear or cloudy based on what enters it. You are not sick because of a broken body. You are living in a body damaged by what you put into it.

03

The Lymphatic System

The lymphatic system has no pump — it moves only through exercise, breathing, and body movement. When it becomes congested, cellular waste backs up into organs and tissues. This congestion is the foundation of virtually every named chronic disease.

04

Parasites — The Hidden Epidemic

Parasitic infections are far more common than acknowledged. A mucus-overloaded body is their ideal habitat. Cleaning out mucus overload and parasites go hand in hand. Natural cleansers: black walnut hull, wormwood, cloves, neem, mimosa pudica seed, pumpkin seed.

05

Melanin & Ancestral Health

Melanin is biologically significant — an absorber of electromagnetic radiation with antioxidant properties. People of African descent have specific nutritional and environmental considerations not addressed by conventional Western medicine, which was largely developed around European bodies.

06

Reclaiming Indigenous Knowledge

Herbs, roots, and plant medicines used for thousands of years by African, Caribbean, and Indigenous peoples represent deep and valid medical knowledge. The goal: reclaim what was taken and integrate it into a whole-person approach to health that Western medicine cannot provide.

The Path Forward

The Healing Path

Healing is not adding a drug to suppress a symptom. Healing is reversing the conditions that caused the problem. If we created the overload, we can stop creating it — and the body will clear itself.

01

Stop the Assault

Remove acid-forming foods — dairy, meat, processed foods, white sugar, white flour. Stop consuming tap water loaded with chlorine, fluoride, and heavy metals. Begin reducing pharmaceutical dependency with professional guidance. When the acid stops, so does the forced mucus production.

02

Hydrate with Living Water

Switch to natural spring water — water that still contains its natural minerals and has not been chemically treated. Proper hydration is the vehicle through which the lymphatic system moves waste. Without it, nothing flows. Aim for one gallon per day.

03

Feed the Electric Body

Transition to alkaline, mineral-rich, plant-based whole foods. Dark leafy greens, wild-grown grains, natural fruits, sea vegetables, and cold-pressed oils. These foods feed the cells at the electrical level and stop forcing the body into a permanent state of mucus production.

04

Move the Lymph Daily

Rebound, walk briskly, swim, breathe deeply, dry brush. The lymph has no pump — you are its pump. Daily movement is not optional for healing; it is the mechanism by which decades of accumulated waste actually gets collected, filtered, and expelled.

05

Fast and Rest

Fasting redirects energy from digestion to deep healing. Autophagy (cellular self-cleaning) accelerates dramatically. The lymphatic system gets a window to finally clear what accumulated over years. Start with intermittent fasting and build under guidance.

06

Cleanse and Rebuild with Herbs

Dr. Sebi's African Bio-Mineral Balance herbs accelerate what lifestyle change begins: sea moss, bladderwrack, burdock root, sarsaparilla, chaparral, elderberry. These are not replacements for changing what you eat — they are the accelerator pedal once you've changed direction.
